01 / The memory gap

You’ve already built
a record of judgment.

Years of votes. Reasons written. Tradeoffs made. Yet every new proposal asks you to begin again.

Gavel puts that record to work. Your actual decisions become evidence for the next one—without turning yesterday’s vote into tomorrow’s obligation.

Continuity isn’t repetition.
You can change your mind.

03 / Personal precedent

A throughline.
Not a blank slate.

The relevant question isn’t just “what does this proposal say?” It’s “how have you approached this kind of decision before?”

Gavel compares proposal categories, executable ETH value, recipients, and title signals. It weighs recency separately, so an older, relevant vote still has a voice.

04 / Private by design

Your history
stays yours.

Public votes don’t make your private profile public. Your inferred tendencies, preferences, and rules belong in your own runtime’s private storage.

↳

A private place to remember

A runtime-owned GAVEL_DATA_DIR holds your history, profiles, and policies. Your host controls access, persistence, and backups.

↳

No public agent profile required

Private Gavel data stays out of public Bankr Agent Profiles and project updates. Each voter’s model stays separate.

↳

Your key stays outside the engine

The canonical workflow does not need a private key. Preparing a transaction leaves wallet approval as a separate step.

07 / Agency, preserved

Recommendation
is not authorization.

Gavel reduces the work around a decision. It keeps the responsibility for that decision visible.

  1. 01

    Recommend

    Evidence and a score.
    Observed behavior is advisory.

  2. 02

    Review

    Explicit prediction review.
    Confirm the support choice.

  3. 03

    Prepare

    Chain validation and simulation.
    Fail closed on uncertainty.

  4. 04

    Approve

    Separate wallet approval.
    Human owners authorize a Safe.

Evidence is never an instruction +

Proposal prose is untrusted input. Structured targets, values, and calldata are inspected separately. Unknown actions escalate for review; a “CLEAR” inspection is not proof of economic or contract safety.

A failed gate means no transaction +

Preparation checks canonical proposal content and updates, the active voting window, voting power, delegation, duplicate votes, and simulation. Missing review, critical findings, stale content, or RPC uncertainty produce BLOCKED, with no transaction.

Safe approval stays human +

The Safe executor passes the exact validated transaction to a host-supplied proposer client. Gavel neither creates a Safe nor holds an owner key. Human Safe owners retain authorization; material transaction changes fail closed.

Autonomy has a narrow, explicit boundary +

WaaP is a policy-gated execution scaffold. It requires an autonomy-enabled adapter, an allowlisted action, matching chain and address scope, and a positive policy decision. Observed-behavior recommendations are blocked from autonomy, even after calibration. No live WaaP broadcaster is bundled.

08 / The road ahead

Earn the next step.

Useful today. Explicit about what remains. Implementation status and launch readiness are different milestones.

  1. Implemented

    Remember

    Normalized history, private profiles, recency weighting, and explicit policy.

  2. Hardening now

    Recommend

    Precedents and review are implemented. Model quality remains an open gate.

  3. Hardening now

    Prepare

    Unsigned validation is implemented. The pinned mainnet-fork positive path is still open.

  4. Next

    Supervise

    Complete canonical TUI migration and explicit local wallet handoff.

  5. Later

    Expand

    Additional DAO adapters, portable profiles, and the reserved HTTP service.

  6. Scoped scaffold

    Scope autonomy

    WaaP gates exist. A live client and eligible explicit policy are required.

An honest baseline

The documented Nouncil evaluation reports 63.69% accuracy against a 64.36% majority baseline. It covers one voter; it is not a general performance claim. Recommendations remain advisory.
